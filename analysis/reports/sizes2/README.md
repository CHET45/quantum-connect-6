# New board sizes: rc8, rc8-wide, rc7, rc5 (2026-11-05/06)

Real protocol (MatchMain, 100 ms per call, 80 ms bots, default constructor jars from `qc-bot/dist/night/`), random 2-ply openings, seed 91 (reruns seed 207). P lane = CPUs 0-11, E lane = mask FF000. Script `night/tools/sizes2.sh`, summary `night/tools/sizes2-sum.py`, logs `night/logs/sizes2/`. Only sizes never tested before.

**Faults:** about 1200 games, 0 crashes, 0 invalid columns or specials, 3 first-call timeouts (6x6 one, 4x50 two). The second (special) call reaches 100-104 ms at p99.99 on narrow boards: the known limit probe, costs at most a blast.

**30 s rule:** MatchMain does not stop games at 30 s. ">370 plies" counts games longer than about 30 s at ~81 ms per ply; on the server those are draws. On 3x100, 60x8, 100x6, 6x100 more than half the games are that long.

**Reruns (more pairs):**
- 3x100 rc8 vs rc7: 54.4% of 40 pairs, +30 [-15, +77]; with 30 s rule 48.8% +-3.5. The first run's 37.5% was noise. No regression.
- 100x50 rc8-wide vs rc8: 42.5% of 30 pairs; with the first run 39% of 40 pairs (30 s rule 40% +-10). rc8-wide is probably worse on tall boards with more than 40 columns.
- 11x13 rc8 vs rc5: 44.4% of 40 pairs, -39 [-98, +17]; with the first run about 44% of 52 pairs. On boards up to 40 columns rc8 = rc7. Fits earlier rc7 vs rc5 on 8x12 (48.3% of 60) and 9x9 here (45.8%): on small and medium boards rc7/rc8 are not better than rc5, maybe slightly worse. Suspect: `Options.partial` (rc7), which scored 46.5% on 15x11 and 47.5% on 8x12 in `logs/e-partial-sizes`. Not tested.
